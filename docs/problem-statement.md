# Problem Statement

## 1. What problem are we solving?

Businesses often need to make difficult decisions, such as whether to launch a new product or enter a new market. The information needed to make these decisions is usually spread across different sources, making research and analysis time-consuming and difficult.

We want to build a system that can help answer complex business questions by researching information, analyzing evidence, and producing a structured decision report.

## 2. Who will use this system?

* Founders
* CEOs
* Business managers
* Decision-makers

## 3. What will the user give to the system?

The user will provide a complex business question.

Example:

> Should our company launch a food delivery business in Hyderabad?

## 4. What should the system eventually return?

The system should return an evidence-based decision report containing:

* Recommendation
* Key findings
* Supporting evidence
* Risks
* Assumptions
* Confidence level
* Sources

## 5. Why is this better than doing the research manually?

Manual research can take a significant amount of time because information is scattered across multiple sources. It can also be difficult to compare evidence, identify important information, and build a structured conclusion.

The system aims to reduce research time and provide a structured, evidence-based analysis that helps decision-makers make better-informed decisions.
