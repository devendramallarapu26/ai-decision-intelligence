# System Design

## 1. System Goal

The system accepts a complex business question and produces an evidence-based business decision report. It is designed as a business decision-support system, not as a general chatbot.

## 2. User Input

The system accepts a complex business question from a founder, CEO, manager, or other decision-maker.

## 3. Planner

The planner breaks the main business question into smaller research questions or tasks that need to be answered.

## 4. Researcher

The researcher works on each sub-question and gathers relevant information from available sources.

## 5. Evidence System

The system stores the important evidence and records the sources supporting the information so that the final conclusions can be traced back to their sources.

## 6. Analysis

The system analyzes the collected evidence and identifies important findings, patterns, opportunities, and risks.

## 7. Decision

The system uses the analysis to generate a business recommendation based on the available evidence.

## 8. Final Report

The system presents a structured business report containing the recommendation, key findings, evidence, risks, assumptions, and relevant market information.
